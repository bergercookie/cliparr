# Cliparr

Save the moments of your Jellyfin library that you want to see again, with your own quote, note and tags, and replay them on a loop.

Cliparr is a small self-hosted server. You log in with your Jellyfin account, pick a movie or episode, mark where the moment starts and ends, and it shows up on your clips page. The video itself keeps streaming from Jellyfin.
