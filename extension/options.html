Cliparr for Jellyfin

Adds a bookmark button to Jellyfin's video player and saves the moments to your Cliparr server.

The address you open Jellyfin with in this browser.

The address of your Cliparr server. You need no separate login: the extension uses your Jellyfin login.

Using it

  1. Open a movie or episode in Jellyfin and play it.
  2. Click Bookmark (or press B) where the moment starts, play on, and click again where it ends.
  3. Shift+click or Shift+B saves the last 10 seconds in one step. Esc cancels a start mark.
